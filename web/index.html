How to Play

Quake III Arena is the fastest deathmatch ever made, running in your browser via the open-source ioquake3 engine and OpenArena. You're dropped into the arena with bots. Frag everything. Highest score when the clock runs out wins the round.

Controls

  • Click the arena once to grab your mouse
  • WASD move · Space jump · mouse aims, LMB fires
  • Number keys 1–9 switch weapons — grab them off the floor
  • Tab shows the scoreboard · Esc opens the menu

Scoring

  • Frag a bot — +1 · get fragged — nothing · fall in the void — −1
  • Your run posts automatically when the match ends. Best run of the round counts.
  • Add a wallet to your profile to appear on the paid leaderboard.

Tips

  • Never stop moving. Strafe-jump to build speed.
  • Control the armor and the rocket launcher — the map cycles them on timers.
  • The railgun rewards patience; the lightning gun rewards tracking.
